Two Years, Countless Memories


“A journey of friendship, growth, and unforgettable moments.”

Our two years as HUMSS B were filled with laughter, friendship, challenges, and unforgettable memories. We may have started as classmates, but along the way, we became a family who supported and helped one another through different experiences. We are also thankful to our teachers, Madam Sol and Ms. Gen, for guiding us, teaching us, and being part of our journey. Their patience, lessons, and encouragement made our time together even more meaningful. This picture is more than just a class photo. It represents the memories, friendships, and moments that we will carry with us even after we leave HUMSS B. No matter where life takes us, we will always remember the two wonderful years we shared together.


[OUR MEMORIES]

My two years in Senior High School were filled with countless memories that I will always treasure. From meeting new friends and sharing laughter in the classroom to facing challenges, completing projects, and learning valuable lessons, every moment became part of my journey. There were times when things felt difficult, but having friends and classmates by my side made those moments easier and more meaningful. These two years were not just about studying and preparing for the future they were also about growing, discovering myself, and creating memories that I will carry with me. As I look back on my SHS days, I realize that two years may seem short, but they were enough to create countless memories that will last a lifetime.
